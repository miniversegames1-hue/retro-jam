Privacy Policy

Retro Jam is developed by Semih Gülücü (Miniverse Games). This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad and Watch and this website. Contact: miniverse.games1@gmail.com. Updated: 24 October 2026.

Game sessions, personal records, achievements, preferences and your customized avatar are stored on your devices. Paired devices exchange game progress and settings through WatchConnectivity. The developer does not operate an account system or a server storing this progress. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Game Center is optional. When connected, only scores earned in phone gameplay are submitted to Game Center leaderboards when available; Watch scores remain personal records. Apple processes Game Center sign-in and App Store purchases or restoration. We do not receive payment card information. Apple services and backups follow Apple’s privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data; paired devices and Apple-managed backups may retain separate copies. To remove those copies, manage the app and backups on the relevant devices. Support email does not remotely erase device data.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot, not for advertising or sale. We retain correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Contact us to request access or deletion of your support correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and standard request logs under its privacy statement. This site uses no forms, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. Contact us if a child has sent personal information through support. Changes to this policy will be dated here.
