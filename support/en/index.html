Support

Play on your phone or Watch. Open a game’s guide to learn its touch or Crown controls. Games can be played offline; purchases, restoration and world rankings require internet. Only phone scores contribute to world rankings.

Three games are free; other available games belong to the same one-time unlock purchase. Restore purchases from Settings using the Apple account used for purchase. Price and availability are shown by the App Store; no subscription is required.

For connection issues, keep your paired devices nearby, open Retro Jam on the Watch and use Help → Sync again. Local records stay on the device while disconnected. For help, email miniverse.games1@gmail.com with your device model, OS/app version and reproduction steps. Screenshots are optional. Never send passwords or payment details.
